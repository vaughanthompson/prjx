Introduction. By Jason Steele.


Welcome to the August edition of the TDX/LDX newsletter.


The stories in this edition are great examples of our purpose in action: it's our people who keep our customers going. They also demonstrate how our values guide the way we support one another, work with our partners and deliver for customers.

You'll read about the continued development of our Shell and Aftersales Territory Manager teams. Our focus on listening to customers and connecting them with the wider TDX offering reflects our belief that customer success is our business. The way the teams managed recent supply challenges also showed what it means to Commit and Deliver—communicating openly and finding practical solutions.

Our Quarry Conference feature highlights the strength of our partnerships with Volvo, our customers and the wider industry. The support of our lower South Island team also demonstrated the value of being In It Together.

The Southern Screen Works story shows what can be achieved when we understand a customer's operation, build a strong relationship and do what we say we will do. We also recognise Tom Fredrickson, whose technical expertise, knowledge-sharing and support for his colleagues are delivering excellent outcomes for both our team and our customers.

Safety remains our number one priority and is at the heart of our Take Care value. A safety survey will be coming soon, and I encourage everyone to have their say. We want to understand what we're doing well and where we can improve. If we see something, we need to say something and do something.

Finally, keep the nominations coming for our Trip of a Lifetime competition, thanks to Donaldson Filtration. Four TDX or LDX employees will join me on an all-expenses-paid trip to Sydney, including the NRL Grand Final, time around Darling Harbour and a tour of Donaldson Australia's filter factory.

We've already received some inspiring nominations recognising people who bring our purpose and values to life. If someone you work with consistently Takes Care, Commits and Delivers, works well with others or goes above and beyond to keep our customers going, please nominate them.

Thank you for everything you do. I hope you enjoy the newsletter.

Jason Steele
General Manager - Shell & Aftersales Support

 

What does 'Take Care' mean to you?


This month, we asked Mick Skelton and Jason Steele to share what Take Care means to them.


Mick Skelton:

For me, Take Care means looking out for each other every day and making sure everyone goes home in the same condition they arrived—safe, healthy, and ready to enjoy time with their family and friends. Safety isn't just about following procedures; it's about caring for your work mates. If you spot a hazard, notice a risk, or see someone who needs a hand, speak up and take action. Every one of us has a role to play in creating a workplace where people feel safe, supported, and valued. When we take care of each other, we strengthen our team and ensure everyone gets home safely at the end of the day.

Jason Steele:

It's not about me… my view of safety is about those around me whom it impacts.

With my own personal safety, I think about the impact an injury or serious harm would have on my wife and 3 sons. When I look at our staff and those around them, I think of their family and how they would be impacted.

Growing our Shell and Aftersales Focus


There's been plenty happening across the Shell and Aftersales teams over the past 12-months, with a clear goal in mind: getting closer to our customers, understanding what they need and making sure we're bringing the full strength of TDX's products and services to the table.


At the heart of this has been the creation of the Shell and Aftersales Territory Manager (ASTM) role at the end of last year.

“The idea behind the role is straightforward and the focus is really about improving the customer experience while also increasing our share of wallet across the TDX aftersales offering,” said Jason Steele, TDX General Manager - Shell & Aftersales Support.

“We want our people actively talking to customers, identifying opportunities and connecting them with the right products and services.”

That means looking beyond individual products and thinking about the bigger picture of what TDX can offer a customer.

The Shell team has also been reshaped, with a dedicated focus on medium to large Shell Lubricants opportunities in areas that haven't traditionally been core parts of the TDX business. This includes OEM automotive workshops, large marine operations, fleet transport, industrial customers which includes environmental and the power generation sector.

The ASTM team has now grown to six Territory Managers around the country, with the latest starting with us in Napier looking after the lower North Island. Collectively they are bringing together a strong mix of experience and perspectives.

Two team members have come across from the original Shell team, two have moved across from Parts and Service, while two new starters have joined TDX from outside the business and the wider industry. For Jason, that mix is a real strength.

“We've got a great combination of people who understand TDX, our customers and our products, alongside people bringing fresh ideas and experience from outside the business. That gives us a really good platform to build from.”

There's still work to do to refine the model, particularly around processes, applications and consistency, but the early signs are encouraging.

CSA sign-ups are growing, new conversations are taking place around Dafo fire suppression, and there have been some significant wins in wear parts, including undercarriage and cutting edges. Perhaps most importantly, customers are noticing the difference.

“The feedback has been really positive. Quite simply, we have more salespeople talking to our customers every day than we've ever had before. That creates more opportunities to understand what they need and show them what else TDX can do.”



The Shell and ASTM teams have also had to deal with a significant challenge this year, with Shell lubricant shortages resulting from the conflict in the Middle East.

Managing the disruption required plenty of communication, planning and customer support. The teams worked hard to keep customers informed, manage expectations and find practical solutions while supply was constrained. Jason says the way the teams responded has been a real positive.

“It was a challenging period, but the team did a fantastic job of staying close to customers and communicating with them. That really matters when supply is difficult.”

And there has been an unexpected upside. As supply conditions have improved, customers have been very complimentary about the support they received. In fact, several customers using competitor products have now approached TDX to find out how they can work with us.

“It's often said you should never let a crisis pass without looking for the opportunity, and I think the team has done exactly that. We got the communication right, stayed close to our customers and supported them through a difficult time. We're now starting to see the results.”

Ultimately, the ASTM model is about creating a stronger connection between our customers, our branches and the wider range of support TDX can provide.

Having Territory Managers actively looking for opportunities means we can be more proactive rather than simply responding when a customer comes to us.

“The purpose is pretty simple. We want to make it easier for customers to access the full range of TDX products and services, while giving our branches another layer of support in the market.”

That could mean lubricants, parts, wear products, fire suppression, generators or other aftersales solutions. The opportunity is to understand the customer's operation and then bring the right solution to them.

There is still plenty to learn and refine, but the direction is clear.

“We're still developing the model, but the early runs on the board are really encouraging. The team is gaining confidence, we're getting better coverage around the country and, most importantly, customers are responding positively. If the early results are anything to go by, there is plenty more to come.”

Quarry Conference 2026: Connecting TDX with the Industry


The annual Quarry Conference is one of the biggest events on the TDX calendar, and this year's event in Invercargill once again highlighted just how important it is for connecting with customers, suppliers and the wider quarrying industry.


The conference has been running for more than 30 years, with TDX a major supporter for more than a decade. As well as being a key partner of the event, TDX is also a major sponsor of the awards night.

For Northern Sales Manager Nigel Davies, the conference provides a level of industry engagement that is difficult to match.

“This is by far and away the best engagement we have with the quarrying industry. We're talking directly to all the right people, in the right environment, and the feedback we get from our involvement is fantastic.”

That involvement also gives TDX plenty of kudos across the industry.

The 2026 conference attracted approximately 800 participants, which is a particularly strong turnout given the event was held in Invercargill. With the city requiring many attendees to travel significant distances, and accommodation options being more limited than in larger centres, getting that many people together is no small achievement.

The conference moves around the country, with the South Island hosting the event one year followed by two years in the North Island. Next year, it will be Wellington's turn.

For TDX, the Quarry Conference is about much more than simply having a stand at an industry event. It provides an opportunity to have meaningful conversations with existing and potential customers and understand what is happening across the sector.

It's also a natural fit for Volvo Construction Equipment, with Volvo machines highly regarded throughout the quarrying industry.

“It's an incredibly enjoyable industry to be part of, and it's a great place to sell Volvo machines. The product is very well regarded and respected in the quarrying sector.”

Volvo's reputation for efficiency, longevity and operator comfort continues to be an important part of those conversations, while the conference also provides an opportunity to talk about the latest developments in machine technology and powertrains.

“We get the chance to talk to customers about where the industry is heading, what they're looking for and the advancements we're seeing in powertrains and machine technology. Those conversations are really valuable.”

TDX also received a fantastic level of support from Volvo for this year's event, including Maria Constanza Vitorgan Barrenechea, Senior Installation Engineer - Electrical Leader WLO Emob platform.




Having Maria attend all the way from Sweden gave customers the opportunity to hear directly from the people involved in developing the machines and technology they operate every day.

“The level of support we get from Volvo is fantastic and having Maria participate in the event demonstrates the commitment Volvo has to the market and gives us another great opportunity to engage with customers.”

With almost 800 industry participants in attendance, the conference provided plenty of opportunities to strengthen existing relationships, meet potential new customers and showcase the latest Volvo technology.



For TDX, its long-standing involvement with the Quarry Conference continues to deliver strong value and reinforces the importance of being present and actively involved in the industries we serve.

“The Quarry Conference gives us incredible bang for our buck. We're right in front of the people we need to be talking to, we get to spend real time with them. That's what makes this event so valuable for TDX.

“I'd also like to make special mention of Mark Hopwood and the rest of the lower South Island team for their help and work on setting the event up, their support was invaluable and contributed to the outstanding success of the conference.”

Southern Screenworks transitioning to a Volvo fleet


Southern Screenworks is a mobile screening and crushing company based in Canterbury which had similar origins to TDX - a couple of mates who set up a business and are going from strength to strength.


They have been reengaging with TDX and transitioning their fleet to Volvo machines and this year alone we have delivered five machines. This includes a mix of dump trucks, wheel loaders and excavators, with another due before Christmas.

The team has really taken to the Volvo product. They love the productivity, efficiency and safety elements of the new machines.

What's become clear is that they are really enjoying working with TDX, which starts with the relationship - knowing and understanding their business and what they need, then exceeding expectations.
